Company Profile

Wasser Polymer Pvt. Ltd., is an ISO 9001:2008 certified organization engaged in manufacturing, trading and supplying Paint Chemicals and Paints. Our organization holds expertise in manufacturing a trusted quality range of Chemicals and Paints, since 2011. Our product range includes Paint Chemicals, Exterior and Interior Paints, Fire Poof Paint, Epoxy Coatings, Polyurethane Coatings, Heat Resistant Coatings, Miscellaneous Coatings and others. The range offered by us is made using excellent quality ingredients that are procured from some of the most trusted vendors of the industry, most of which is of foreign origin.

Our collection is widely admired by the clients for its aesthetic appeal, abundant color schemes & resistance against fungus that can ruin the walls. Our strength lies in highly trained manpower with rich industry experience in the latest texture technologies and development tools. The entire range of products is designed as per the client's preference and requirements,& is exported to the Indian Subcontinent, Middle East & South East Asia.

Wasser Polymer is led by a team of seasoned professionals with exceptional qualifications and a strong track record of success in their respective domains. The company places a strong emphasis on research & development,continually innovating new products and enhancing existing ones. This commitment is backed by a state-of-the-art laboratory & a skilled team of trained personnel.
